Returns & Refunds FAQ

We want you to love your equipment. Here is how returns, refunds and warranty claims work at Bramble Bakery Supplies.

How long do I have to return an item?

You can return most unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Commercial ovens and mixers have a 14-day window because of freight costs.

Do you refund shipping?

Original shipping is refunded when the return is due to our error or a faulty product. For change-of-mind returns you cover return postage.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are processed within 3 working days of the item arriving at our warehouse and appear on your statement within 5–10 working days depending on your bank.

Can I exchange instead of refunding?

Yes. Choose "Exchange" in the returns portal and we will dispatch the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return.

What does the warranty cover?

All equipment carries a 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Wear items such as mixer bowls and blades are excluded.

How to start a return

  1. Sign in and open Orders.
  2. Select the item and choose Return or exchange.
  3. Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any Collect+ point.
